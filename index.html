Mirror Maze

Can you guide the lasers through the maze and reach every matching crystal?

How to Play

  1. Goal: Guide each colored laser to its matching crystal.
  2. Clues: Numbers show how many mirrors belong in each row and column.
  3. Mirrors: Place / or \ mirrors to redirect lasers 90°.
  4. Blockers: Avoid black squares. Lasers cannot pass through them.
  5. Crossing: Different colored lasers can cross safely.
  6. Fire: Press FIRE LASERS to test your solution.
  7. Lives & Hints: You have 3 lives. Wrong attempts cost 1 life. Use hints if needed.
  8. Connect all lasers correctly to win!

Remember: You can move and rotate mirrors as many times as you want before firing!
